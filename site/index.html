TFT Archipelago

Teamfight Tactics, but you have to earn your rerolls.

An Archipelago randomizer for TFT. Rerolls, XP, augments and shop slots are items in your multiworld.

Download for Windows Free and open source · Doesn't modify the game

Everything starts at zero

No rerolls, no XP buys, one augment choice, three shop slots. Items from the multiworld raise your limits, and click blockers cover whatever is still locked. Go over a limit with hotkeys and that match sends no checks.

Checks are boards

Finish Top 4 with 7 Blossom active, field a 2-star Caitlyn, hit level 8. The tracker lists every check, and you can pin three of them to the in-game overlay.

Setup

  1. Install the app from the latest release. SmartScreen will warn you because it isn't signed yet: More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Create your YAML on the app's Setup page.
  3. Generate with the Archipelago Launcher (0.6.5+) and tft_archipelago.apworld, then host on archipelago.gg.
  4. Connect in the app and queue Normal, Ranked or Tocker's Trials.

Full guide: setup_en.md

FAQ

Can I get banned?
Very unlikely. The app doesn't touch the game, its memory or your input. It only reads the screen, the Riot client's local API and log files, the same way overlays and stat trackers do. It also never gives you an advantage, it only takes options away. Riot hasn't officially reviewed it though, so there's no guarantee.
What is Archipelago?
A multi-game randomizer: your items end up in other players' games and theirs in yours. See archipelago.gg.
